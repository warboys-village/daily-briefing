The town’s first bridge was built of wood in 1107, before work began on a stone replacement in the early 15th century. The stone crossing was completed in 1425, and a chapel was added the following year. It is one of only three bridges in England with a chapel. The bridge also still bears the scars of the English Civil War. Parliamentary troops destroyed part of it to hinder Royalist forces, so a temporary drawbridge was installed. When the damaged section was rebuilt in 1716, it was given rounded arches instead of matching the pointed medieval ones, leaving a visible difference today. He is alleged to have spent £935.83 on personal items using a company credit card while on leave between August 21 and September 2, knowing the transactions were unauthorised and intending to make financial gain. He is also accused of stealing an Apple iPhone worth £285, a Lenovo laptop worth £478 and several tools, including a drill set. The items belonged to water treatment supplier Prodose Ltd and were allegedly stolen between September 2 and October 14. Between November 13 and December 8, Mr Stokes is accused of stealing tools valued at £2,109.18 from Riverside Waste Machinery Ltd. A further fraud charge alleges that he used or permitted the use of company credit cards for unauthorised transactions totalling £412.41 between October 6 and December 8.
