Plans have been submitted to turn a historic outbuilding beside a Grade I listed building into a five-bedroom home. Mick Hilless has applied to Huntingdonshire District Council to turn a barn and office building linked to Bodsey House into a home on Bodsey Toll Road, Ramsey. Bodsey House is Grade I listed, meaning it is of exceptional interest. The property was once a 13th-century hermitage belonging to Ramsey Abbey but has since been altered and turned into a family home. The plans would turn the barn and office building at the rear of the site into one five-bedroom home. The historic barn is curtilage listed, which means it is legally protected as it sits within the grounds of a listed building. Documents by Scroxton Architects say the barn and office building are dilapidated and no longer suitable for modern agricultural or commercial use.
