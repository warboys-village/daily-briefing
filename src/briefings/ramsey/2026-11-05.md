    <div><span class="badge-status badge-other">4 Nov</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="news-summary">
    <p>A Huntingdonshire man is due to appear in court accused of a string of fraud and theft offences. Matthew Stokes, 35, of Liberator Road, Ramsey, is accused of fraud by false representation and theft by an employee. His first hearing is due to take place on November 5 at Cambridge Magistrates' Court. He is alleged to have spent £935.83 on personal items using a company credit card while on leave between August 21 and September 2, knowing the transactions were unauthorised and intending to make financial gain. He is also accused of stealing an Apple iPhone worth £285, a Lenovo laptop worth £478 and several tools, including a drill set. The items belonged to water treatment supplier Prodose Ltd and were allegedly stolen between September 2 and October 14. Between November 13 and December 8, Mr Stokes is accused of stealing tools valued at £2,109.18 from Riverside Waste Machinery Ltd. A further fraud charge alleges that he used or permitted the use of company credit cards for unauthorised transactions totalling £412.41 between October 6 and December 8. All the offences are alleged to have taken place in Huntingdon.</p>
