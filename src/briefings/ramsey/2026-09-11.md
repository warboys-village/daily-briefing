  </div>
  <div class="news-summary">
    <p>A county council report revealed new 20mph limits on roads have shown “statistically significant” drops in speed a year after being introduced. Cambridgeshire County Council began introducing the new limits across the county from early 2024, across a total of 25 roads so far. The report, presented to the council’s highways and transport committee on Tuesday (September 1), looked at the impact of the limit on eight of these roads within the first year. It found that average speed was reduced on all of them. It looked at schemes in Cambourne, Histon, Ely, Great Abington, Fowlmere, Ramsey, St Neots, and Great Staughton. Speed reductions ranged from six per cent to 16 per cent, with the largest falls seen where speeds were highest before the limit was introduced. The report said early evidence of the limit’s impact on crashes is “encouraging but remains inconclusive” as numbers were already low in many towns.
